Delivery Charge Setting

These figures set how delivery fees are calculated across every shipment this company books — from the Vendor portal, the Branch portal, and the dispatcher's own "New shipment" form.
Base fee + (per KM or per KG × distance/weight), then multiplied for Express or Same Day service types. Tax is applied when generating invoices.

Shipment Status Setting

Rename or recolor how each delivery stage is shown — in status pills, status filters, and the shipment timeline — across the Vendor, Branch, and Admin portals. The stages themselves (and what each one unlocks, like assigning a rider or marking something received) stay the same; only the label and color shown for each one change.

Custom Statuses

Add your own extra statuses — like "On Hold" or "Customs Check" — that staff can apply to a shipment as a note alongside its normal delivery stage. These don't affect assigning riders, advancing, or branch transfers.
